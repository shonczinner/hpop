## Key Findings

1. **Multifamily share is the universal predictor** of the HPOP/Owner-Occ gap — higher multifamily → smaller gap (less Owner-Occ > HPOP). r = -0.662 (MS) and -0.926 (NYC).

2. **Gap sign flips**: state-level MS shows Owner-Occ > HPOP, but dense urban NYC PUMAs show Owner-Occ ≤ HPOP due to high multifamily concentration.

3. **Rent-to-income at PUMA level**: r = -0.19 (MS) and 0.25 (NYC). Weaker than at state level, suggesting rent burden is more of a regional than neighborhood phenomenon.

4. **NY state vs NYC PUMAs**: New York state's multifamily share (46.5%) sits below most NYC PUMAs (25.2%–98.6%), reflecting upstate's lower density.
